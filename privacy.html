Privacy PolicyHARPER CALENDARSYNC

1. Scope

This policy covers Harper CalendarSync ("the App"), a personal, non-commercial tool that accesses Google Calendar data through the Google Calendar API. The App is used by one individual and is not offered to the public.

2. Data we collect

  • Your Google account email address — used only to identify the authorized account
  • Google Calendar contents — event titles, times, locations and attendees, limited to the account you authorized

3. How the data is used

The data above is used solely to read and create calendar entries on your own device. It is not used for analytics, profiling, advertising, or any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

  • OAuth credentials are stored only on your own local device
  • There is no server-side database; calendar data is never uploaded to any third party
  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties

5. Third parties

The only third party involved is Google LLC (Google Calendar API), governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Your rights

  • You may revoke the App's access at any time from Google account third-party access
  • Once revoked, the App can no longer read your calendar data

7. Contact

For questions, please contact the user support email address shown on the Google consent screen for this application.

8. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page. Last updated: 22 September 2026.
